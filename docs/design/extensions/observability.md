# Extension — observability

Intent: full explanatory coverage of infrastructure, controllers, agents, providers, decisions, cost and effects through OpenTelemetry traces, metrics and logs, with ClickHouse as the analytics and retention backend — a side channel that never authorizes anything and is never the only copy of a learning record (THESIS goal 7, I-9).
Gate: outcome and usage records independent of any sink, a stubbed missing sink, and canary redaction at **M0-Q**; the pipeline, redaction, identity, backfill, retention and alerting at **G-OBS**.

Relies on (kernel): the canonical-record obligation and gap creation (KERNEL §8, F-32); audit events and projections as non-authority (KERNEL §1, F-6); the telemetry-isolation rule of the trust model.

Decisions:

- **Telemetry explains; it never authorizes, blocks or completes workflow**, and a span, metric or log is never accepted as evidence of a transition. `OutcomeRecord`, `UsageReceipt` and `TelemetryGap` are not telemetry; they are aggregates created under the kernel's obligation, so a sink, collector or query-store outage creates a gap and never removes a denominator.
- **Two collector tiers**: node agents (SDK-side redaction, Kubernetes identity, bounded queues) and gateways (defense-in-depth redaction, policy routing, tail sampling, batching, retry, export). Separate pipelines for traces, metrics and logs; pinned collector and exporter versions; exporter upgrade and data-loss tests before production acceptance.
- **Redaction is fail-closed and happens in the SDK before any queue or WAL**; a failed, bypassed or schema-uncertain redactor rejects or quarantines the record with `RedactionFailed`. Gateway redaction is defense in depth, never the first line.
- **Telemetry identity is `(installation_id, boot_epoch, source_sequence)`**; a stale epoch is rejected from current evidence; a gap in a source sequence is a visible observation gap. A missing trace is never evidence that something did not happen.
- **Sampling keeps what matters**: SLO metrics are never sampled away; errors, safety violations, ambiguous effects, low-confidence or overridden decisions, provider outages, session exhaustion, acceptance failures and custody incidents are retained; routine success is sampled, and sampling rises during incidents. A late error upgrades and retains its trace and emits a canonical correction; it cannot retroactively change authority or complete a cohort.
- **Metric labels are bounded**; high-cardinality identities live in traces, logs and canonical records.
- **ClickHouse is a rebuildable, deduplicated, versioned projection** with policy-controlled retention, tenant authorization, TTL, migration, compliance holds and query limits. Artifact references carry tenant, owner, KMS key, access class, retention policy and access-audit identity.
- **Degradation is visible and bounded**: `TelemetryHealthy | TelemetryDegraded | CollectorUnavailable | ExportBacklogged | ClickHouseUnavailable | TelemetryPolicyBlocked | TelemetryRecovered` are conditions on the telemetry capability; buffers are bounded with visible drops; a full buffer never blocks the Manager indefinitely; whether degraded telemetry blocks a high-risk action is policy.
- **Policy and objectives are kinds** — `ObservabilityPolicy`, `TelemetryProfile`, `SLO`, `AlertRule`, `TelemetryIncident` — and every SLO or alert declares target, denominator, window, freshness, owner, runbook and an independent alert channel. Minimum SLOs: API write success and staleness, controller queue age, projection lag, materialization and ambiguous-operation age, fence-confirmation age, custody-preservation age, provider availability, decision latency and abstention, settled-cost completeness, gap age, query-store freshness.
