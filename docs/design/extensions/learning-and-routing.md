# Extension — learning and routing

Intent: minimize expected total cost per accepted milestone by learning which worker-strategy configuration to route each class of work to, above the consequence-class floor (THESIS goal 3, I-10).
Gate: the ledger exists from M0-Q; comparison at **G-EVALUATION**; promotion at **G-ADAPT**; production use additionally at **G-PROD**. Until G-ADAPT, routing is one fixed revision.

Relies on (kernel): the routing pin on every `TaskRun` (KERNEL §3.1, F-14); the canonical-record obligation and censoring (KERNEL §8, F-32); the consequence class and floor (ONBOARD §5); reviewer independence and the fixed review tier (ROLES §3); evidence freshness (F-27).

Decisions:

- **Unit of optimization is the execution strategy** — context preparation, worker model and harness, reasoning budget, verification, reviewer, permitted repair rounds, escalation — never the model alone. Its cost includes classification, planning, abandoned attempts, review, CI, repairs, escalation, infrastructure and evaluation overhead; delayed defect cost is reported separately with its uncertainty.
- **The denominator is milestones with a settled acceptance decision at one plan revision.** Task-level records are inputs. Splitting a milestone cannot improve the metric; a revision that splits records the pre-split identity in `superseded_by`.
- **Cost allocation is deterministic and conserved.** A versioned allocation policy attributes direct cost by usage identity, shared platform cost pro rata by `active_taskrun_seconds`, and the unreachable remainder as context `residual`. Per period, attributed components plus residual equal settled plus censored usage. `CostRecord` and `OutcomeAttribution` (one disjoint primary cause from a versioned precedence table, plus contributors) are the records.
- **A cohort is complete only when every assignment's TaskRun has expected records `RECORDED` or `GAP`**, usage settled or censored, outcomes mature and adjudications done. A gap counts as unknown outcome and censored cost; it is never removed. Promotion requires `censored_fraction ≤ max_censored_fraction` with every censored cost at its bound, eligible-but-not-selected records, support bounds, provider-health strata and grouped repository/time splits.
- **Problem classes are learned, versioned and pre-dispatch.** Dimensions: operation, reasoning demand, coupling, specification, verification, novelty, consequence, environment. A class is valid for routing only if recognizable from information available before the decision and predictive on independent cases; a description invented after a failure is diagnostic, not a feature. A taxonomy release pins every definition and question revision.
- **Capability is graded per configuration within a class** — `Unknown | Evaluating | Qualified | Restricted | Suspended` — never as a universal model grade. First-pass worker cohorts, reviewer cohorts and full-strategy cohorts are kept separate; a task accepted after a frontier rewrite is a strategy success, not a first-pass success. Provider outages never downgrade a model.
- **Comparisons are deliberate.** Every routing decision is an immutable `EvaluationAssignment` with candidate set, seed, propensity, stratum, split and policy revision. A shadow decision evaluates the router, not the unexecuted worker. Exploration runs under an escrowed expected-loss budget and stops on unknown cost or support failure.
- **Routing revisions have one lifecycle** — draft, qualified, canary, promoted, rolled back, retired; its machine is printed at G-ADAPT, not here — and promotion or rollback changes only which revision future admissions pin. A canary is a second pin applied to admissions inside its scope. Rollback authority is deterministic triggers (confirmed severity-weighted defects, quality lower-bound breach, censored-fraction breach, support failure) or a human kill switch; in-flight attempts keep their pin. No promotion while an attempt in the canary scope is active under a different revision.
- **The floor is never optimized away.** The router chooses above the consequence-class floor; mandatory review and security checks stay fixed even when a cheaper strategy predicts lower cost without them. Optional review depth becomes adaptive only after its own reliability is demonstrated.
- **Scope discipline and micro-manager decisions are learning signals**: adherence, unauthorized changes, useful findings, false alarms, useful nudges, unnecessary takeovers and missed drift are recorded separately.
